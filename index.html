Fig. 1 · Education

Pramāṇa

A study companion that proves every claim. The name is Sanskrit for a means of valid knowledge.

In
Lecture videos, slide decks and textbooks
Out
A tutor with citations you can click and check, verified quizzes, and a model of what each student knows

Answers split into three lanes: source-backed, outside knowledge and unverified. The quote-lock method checks every quote in code, so a claimed quote that fails is labelled unverified. Off-topic questions are declined before any model call.

Retrieval fuses local embeddings with BM25. Mastery comes from prerequisite-aware Bayesian knowledge tracing. Explanations work in English, Hindi or Hinglish, and evaluation runs RAGAS plus a simulated-student study.

  • Hybrid retrieval
  • Quote-lock
  • Knowledge tracing
  • RAGAS

Fig. 2 · Research

Indic-MIND

Unsupervised, real-time hallucination detection for Hindi, by probing an LLM’s internal states. It adapts the MIND framework (Su et al., ACL 2024 Findings) to a low-resource Indic language.

In
Hidden states from Llama 3.2 3B completing Hindi Wikipedia sentences
Out
A three-layer MLP probe that flags made-up text as it is generated

0.9163 test AUC on Hindi, against 0.9588 for the English control under identical conditions.

The figure shows the idea. True and hallucinated activations, in red, drift apart layer by layer. The points are illustrative.

  • Llama 3.2
  • Hidden-state probing
  • Low-resource NLP

Fig. 6 · Vibracoustic, Jun–Dec 2025

NVH sensor pipelines

NVH means noise, vibration and harshness. I built scalable ETL pipelines for that sensor data using Microsoft Fabric and PySpark.

In
Raw automotive NVH sensor readings
Out
Validated tables, automated Azure Data Factory workflows and Power BI dashboards for real-time insight

n = 3, m = 4

This is the physics, not client data. Sand on a vibrating plate collects along the lines that stay still, known as Chladni figures.

Fig. 7 · Robotics

Precision pick-and-place

A simulated 7-DOF Franka Panda arm learns to track, intercept and grasp a moving block on a conveyor, even after the block disappears into a tunnel.

In
Block state from simulation, randomized belt speed, mass and drift
Out
A control policy that generalizes across episodes

20/20 at constant velocity and 30/30 under domain randomization with PPO. The occlusion stage, using RecurrentPPO with an LSTM, is in progress.

In the tunnel, the robot only has a predicted position. That’s the red block.
